Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NihasPicks ("we", "us") collects and uses information when you visit nihaspicks.com (the "Site"). NihasPicks is operated from Italy, and we handle personal data in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who is responsible

The data controller is the operator of NihasPicks. You can reach us at contact@nihaspicks.com.

What we collect

  • Technical data that your browser sends automatically, such as IP address, browser type, device type and pages visited. Our hosting server keeps short-term logs of this for security and to keep the Site running.
  • Advertising measurement data. If you click "Accept" on our cookie notice, we load the Meta Pixel, which uses cookies to tell us how visitors arrive from our ads and which pages they view, and helps Meta show our ads to people likely to be interested. If you decline, the Meta Pixel is not loaded.
  • Messages you send us by email, if you contact us.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not collect payment details. Purchases are made on the retailer's own website under that retailer's privacy policy.

Why we use it (legal basis)

  • Running and securing the Site — our legitimate interest.
  • Advertising measurement with the Meta Pixel — your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Answering your messages — our legitimate interest in replying to you.

Affiliate links

When you click a link to a retailer, the link passes through our affiliate network (MaxBounty) so the retailer can record that you came from our Site. The network and the retailer may set their own cookies. Please read their privacy policies.

Third parties

We use: our hosting provider (Hetzner Online GmbH, Germany); Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. for the Meta Pixel (only with your consent); and MaxBounty for affiliate tracking. Some of these may process data outside the EU under appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep data

Server logs are kept for a short period for security purposes. Emails are kept as long as needed to deal with your request.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to or restrict its use, or withdraw consent. To withdraw cookie consent, clear this site's data in your browser and choose "Decline" when the notice appears again. You may also complain to your local data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

Children

The Site is intended for adults shopping for gifts. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
